## Introduction

[MakerDAO](https://makerdao.com/en/) is a decentralized autonomous organization managing and issuing the **stablecoin** DAI, which is designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with the US Dollar.

> **Update (November 2026).** MakerDAO has been renamed [Sky](https://sky.money). The same core contracts still issue DAI, and since September 2024 they also issue **USDS**, a 1:1 successor token. A new section, [Versions, from Sai to Sky](#versions-from-sai-to-sky), lists what each version of the protocol introduced and describes the current mainnet configuration. Statements in the rest of the article now say which version they refer to.

### Dai principle

Unlike traditional fiat-backed stablecoins that rely on a centralized entity to hold reserves, Dai is generated through a process called **collateralized debt positions (CDPs)**. Users lock up collateral, typically in the form of Ethereum (ETH), in a smart contract, and in return, they receive Dai. This process ensures that Dai is always backed by collateral, maintaining its stability.

Since the Peg Stability Module (2020), part of the DAI supply is minted against USDC deposited at a 1:1 rate rather than against volatile collateral, so DAI is also partly backed by a centralised stablecoin.

## General concept

### Collateralized Debt Positions (CDPs)

CDPs are at the core of MakerDAO's operation. When a user wants to generate Dai, they create a CDP by locking up a certain amount of collateral. The collateral is held in a smart contract, and based on the value of the collateral, a user can generate a specific amount of Dai. This mechanism allows users to access liquidity without having to sell their assets, providing a decentralized alternative to traditional lending platforms.

In Multi-Collateral DAI a CDP is called a **vault**: an `urn` of a collateral type (`ilk`) in the `Vat`. Its debt is `art · rate` (normalised debt times the fee accumulator of the ilk), and it is safe while `art · rate ≤ ink · spot`, where `ink` is the locked collateral and `spot` the collateral price divided by the liquidation ratio `mat`.

### Vaults and Stability Fee

The collateral held in CDPs is stored in MakerDAO's Vaults. 

For the old [Single-Collateral Dai](https://andytudhope.github.io/community/scd-faqs/stability-fee/) version, the mechanism can be described thus

The stability of Dai is maintained by adjusting the stability fee, an interest rate charged on the generated Dai. If the value of the collateral falls, the stability fee is increased, encouraging users to close their CDPs and reduce the circulating supply of Dai. This dynamic system helps to stabilize Dai and ensures that it remains pegged to the US Dollar.

For the new version which introduces the MCD system, you can read the documentation on [maker DAO](https://docs.makerdao.com/smart-contract-modules/rates-module)

In Multi-Collateral DAI the fee is set per collateral type. The `Jug` contract holds a per-second rate `duty` for each ilk plus a global `base`; anyone can call `drip(ilk)`, which multiplies the ilk's `rate` and credits the accrued fee to the `Vow` (the system's balance sheet) as surplus. On mainnet, the rates of many ilks are now moved by a rate-setter module (SP-BEAM) within bounds set by governance, rather than by each executive spell.

Reference: [stability fee](https://andytudhope.github.io/community/scd-faqs/stability-fee/)

### Governance and MKR Token

MakerDAO operates as a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO): protocol parameters are set by token holder vote rather than by a company. Voting runs through the MKR token, the native governance token of the platform. MKR holders have the power to vote on proposals that impact the protocol, such as changes to the stability fee, adding new collateral types, or modifying the parameters of the system.

Since the Sky upgrade, the governance token is **SKY**, and the `MKR_SKY` contract converts MKR into SKY. Token holders vote in the `Chief`; the winning proposal, an executive **spell**, waits behind a pause delay (48 hours in the current configuration) before it runs with the authority of the pause proxy, which holds the admin rights on every core contract.

## Versions, from Sai to Sky

The protocol has been upgraded in steps. The table below gives, for each step, what it introduced and the source used for the date. Dates come from the tags of the [`dss`](https://github.com/sky-ecosystem/dss) repository and from the dated folders of the executive spells archived in [`spells-mainnet`](https://github.com/sky-ecosystem/spells-mainnet); a spell date is the date of the executive vote, not always the date the code was deployed.

| ------- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| Single-Collateral DAI (Sai) | 2017 (general knowledge, code not reviewed) | One collateral (ETH), one CDP contract, the SAI token, a global settlement |
| Multi-Collateral DAI (`dss` 1.0) | tag `1.0.0` dated 2019-11-07, launch spell 2019-11-18 | `Vat` accounting for several collateral types, `Jug` fees, `Pot` (DAI Savings Rate), `Spot`, `Vow`; liquidations by `Cat` + `Flip` English auctions; surplus auctions (`Flap`) and debt auctions (`Flop`); Emergency Shutdown (`End`) |
| Peg Stability Module | spell 2020-12-20 | `PSM-USDC-A`: swap USDC and DAI at 1:1, with a fee in (`tin`) and out (`tout`) |
| Liquidations 2.0 | `dss` commit "Liq 2.0" 2021-04-02, tag `1.3.0` 2021-04-19; first spell 2021-04-19 (LINK-A) | `Dog` + `Clipper` Dutch auctions, partial liquidations, limits `hole`/`Hole`, keeper incentives `tip`/`chip`, circuit breaker |
| Cure | `dss` commit 2022-05-18 | Lets shutdown subtract DAI that is pre-minted but not in circulation |
| LitePSM | DAO resolution spell 2024-07-04, onboarding spell 2024-07-25 | A cheaper PSM over a pool of pre-minted DAI; the old PSM is migrated to it in phases (phase 3 in the 2024-10-04 spell) |
| Sky (Endgame) | spell 2024-09-13 | USDS, sUSDS (initial savings rate 6.25 %), the DAI ⇄ USDS converter, the SKY token and the MKR → SKY converter, the surplus `Splitter` |
| New surplus path | spells 2024-09-27 and 2025-10-30 | `FlapperUniV2SwapOnly` (surplus buys SKY on Uniswap V2), then the `Kicker` |

### Single-Collateral DAI (Sai)

The first version, launched in December 2017 (general knowledge), accepted only ETH as collateral and issued what is now called SAI. Its contracts are not part of the local sources used for this update, so this article does not describe its internals beyond the stability-fee paragraph above. After the Multi-Collateral launch, SAI holders could migrate to DAI (general knowledge).

### Multi-Collateral DAI (dss, 2019)

The `dss` repository ("DAI Stablecoin System") is the core still in use. Its `1.0.0` tag holds `vat.sol`, `jug.sol`, `pot.sol`, `spot.sol`, `cat.sol`, `flip.sol`, `flap.sol`, `flop.sol`, `vow.sol`, `end.sol`, `join.sol` and `dai.sol`. The main changes compared with Sai:

- **Several collateral types**, each an `ilk` with its own liquidation ratio, fee, debt ceiling and minimum debt (`dust`), all accounted for in one contract, the `Vat`.
- **DAI Savings Rate** (`Pot`): DAI deposited earns a rate paid by minting unbacked DAI against the Vow.
- **Liquidations by English auction.** `Cat.bite` seized an unsafe vault and started a `Flipper` auction in two phases: bidders first raised the DAI bid (`tend`) until the debt plus penalty was covered, then lowered the amount of collateral they accepted (`dent`). The code defaults are a 5 % minimum bid step, 3 hours per bid and 2 days at most per auction.
- **Surplus and debt auctions.** The `Vow` sold surplus DAI for MKR, which was burned (`Flap`), and covered bad debt by minting and selling MKR (`Flop`). After the March 2020 crash ("Black Thursday", general knowledge), debt auctions minted MKR to cover the shortfall left by liquidation auctions that cleared at zero bid.
- **Emergency Shutdown.** `End` freezes the system and lets DAI holders redeem a fixed basket of collateral. The Emergency Shutdown Module (`ESM`), whose source is not in `dss`, let MKR holders trigger it without a governance vote by depositing a threshold of MKR. This comes from general knowledge, although the spells of 2024 still set this threshold in MKR.

### Peg Stability Module (2020)

The PSM, onboarded by the 2020-12-20 spell as the `PSM-USDC-A` collateral type, mints DAI against USDC at 1:1 (minus the fee `tin`) and gives USDC back for DAI (plus `tout`). The ilk has a 100 % ratio and no liquidation. It gives DAI a direct exit to USDC, but it also makes a large part of DAI depend on USDC.

### Liquidations 2.0 (2021)

Liquidations 2.0 replaces `Cat` and `Flipper` with `Dog` and `Clipper`:

- `Dog.bark` starts a liquidation, possibly partial when the per-ilk (`hole`) or global (`Hole`) limit on DAI being raised binds.
- The `Clipper` sells the collateral in a **Dutch auction**: the price starts above market (`top = price · buf`) and falls over time along one of the curves of `abaci.sol`: linear, stair-step exponential or continuous exponential. The first buyer at an acceptable price takes the lot at once with `take`; no bidding rounds.
- `take` sends the collateral before payment and can call the buyer back through `clipperCall`, so a keeper can sell the collateral in the same transaction without holding DAI. This is a flash liquidation.
- The keeper who starts an auction gets an incentive `tip + chip · tab`, minted against the Vow.
- `redo` restarts an auction that ran too long (`tail`) or fell too far (`cusp`), and `CLIPPER_MOM` can stop auctions without the pause delay.

The same 2021-04-19 spell deployed a new `End` and a new `ESM` wired to the Dog.

### LitePSM (2024)

The `DssLitePsm` swaps USDC and DAI out of a **pool of pre-minted DAI** in two ERC-20 transfers, instead of touching the Vat on each swap. The USDC sits in a separate `pocket`; permissionless keepers call `fill` to mint more DAI into the pool, `trim` to burn excess DAI and `chug` to send fees to the Vow. `DssLitePsmMom` can halt either direction without the pause delay. Its README states that it assumes Emergency Shutdown can never be called.

### Sky and USDS (2024)

The 2024-09-13 spell initialised the Endgame contracts:

- **USDS**, an ERC-20 behind a UUPS proxy, with its own join contract `UsdsJoin`. Like DAI, it is a claim on the internal DAI balance of the Vat.
- **DaiUsds**, a permissionless 1:1 converter in both directions. Converting does not change the system's total debt.
- **sUSDS**, an ERC-4626 savings token paid the Sky Savings Rate (SSR), the USDS counterpart of the DSR.
- **SKY**, the new governance token, with the `MKR_SKY` converter.
- The surplus **Splitter**, which sends part of the surplus to a SKY farm.

### Current mainnet configuration (November 2026)

From the test configuration of `spells-mainnet` at commit `1f04284` (2026-11-09), which asserts the state after the 2026-11-05 spell:

| Parameter | Value | Effect |
| --------- | ----- | ------ |
| `vow_sump` / `vow_dump` | max / 0 | **Debt auctions are off**: bad debt is no longer covered by minting the governance token, only by the surplus buffer or a governance decision |
| `vow_hump` / `vow_bump` | max / 0 | The Vow's own surplus auction (`flap`) is off |
| Kicker `khump` / `kbump` | -200 M USDS / 10,000 USDS | Surplus leaves through the Kicker into the Splitter and `FlapperUniV2SwapOnly`, which buys SKY on Uniswap V2 (`flap_want` 98 %) |
| `esm_min` | max | The ESM cannot be triggered by token holders: **shutdown is reachable only by governance** through `End.cage` |
| `pause_delay` | 48 hours | Reaction window before a spell runs |
| DSR / SSR | set by SP-BEAM within 0-30 % / 2-30 % | Savings rates move without a full spell |
| ETH-A / ETH-B / ETH-C `mat` | 145 % / 130 % / 170 % | Liquidation ratios of the ETH vault types |

The ESM threshold was raised in steps, to 300,000 MKR in the 2024-07-25 spell and to 500,000 MKR in the 2024-11-28 spell, before reaching its current maximum; the spell that set the maximum, and the ones that switched the Vow auctions off, were not identified in the sources used here. The 2026-11-05 spell also offboarded the GUSD and USDP PSMs.

## Liquidation example

Example with ETH = $3,000 and a collateral ratio required of 1.5

1) Deposit 1 ETH into Maker
1) Borrow 1,000 DAI

Collateral ratio = 3'000 / 1'000 = 3

2. Price of Ether drops to $1,400
   1. Collateral ratio drops to 1.4 due to 1,400 / 1,000 = 1.4 < 1.5
   2. Take my 1 ETH and auction it off (Dutch auction since Liquidations 2.0, English auction before)
   3. Raise enough DAI to pay back my borrow + liquidation fee
   4. Leftover collateral sent back to me, the owner of the liquidated position

The price used is the delayed price of the oracle security module (OSM), so in a fast crash a vault can fall under water before it can be liquidated. With Liquidations 2.0 the penalty is `chop` (13 % for ETH-A) and the auction can be partial when the liquidation limits are reached.

| Peg Stability Module (PSM)                                   | Enables quick swapping  of DAI with other stablecoins at a rate of 1:1 by creating a pooled  collateralized-debt position with 100% collateralization ratio and 0%  stability fees. The USDC PSM now runs on the LitePSM (`DssLitePsm`). |
| [Rates Module](https://docs.makerdao.com/smart-contract-modules/rates-module) | Accumulates stability fees on Vault debt balances, as well as interest on Dai Savings Rate (DSR) deposits. |
| [Shutdown](https://docs.makerdao.com/smart-contract-modules/shutdown) | Stops and gracefully  settles the Maker Protocol while ensuring that all users, both Dai  holders and Vault holders, receive the net value of assets they are entitled to. |
| [System Stabilizer  Module](https://docs.makerdao.com/smart-contract-modules/system-stabilizer-module) | Corrects the system when the value of the collateral backing Dai drops below the liquidation level (determined by governance) when the  stability of the system is at risk. Its debt auctions and the Vow's surplus auctions are switched off in the current configuration. |

## Risks and Challenges

MakerDao faces different challenges:

- Its reliance on Ethereum as the underlying blockchain exposes MakerDAO to network congestion and scalability issues.
- Additionally, the stability of Dai is contingent on the collateral backing it, and extreme market volatility can pose risks to the system. 
- **USDC dependence.** Through the PSM and then the LitePSM, a large share of DAI and USDS is backed by USDC held in the `pocket`. A USDC depeg is absorbed at par, and once the pocket is empty DAI loses its 1:1 exit.
- **Losses with debt auctions off.** An auction that ends with collateral sold out and debt left leaves bad debt in the Vow. With `sump` at its maximum, no debt auction mints the governance token, so only the surplus buffer or a governance action absorbs it.
- **Governance as the only shutdown path.** With the ESM disabled, a majority of SKY votes controls the system, and the 48-hour pause is the only window to react. The "Mom" contracts (oracle, Clipper, debt ceiling, PSM) let governance act without that delay.
- **Upgradeable tokens.** USDS and sUSDS are UUPS proxies, whereas DAI and the Vat are not upgradeable.
