Applied cryptography as it shows up in real systems rather than as pure theory: block cipher modes, elliptic curves and the discrete logarithm problem, hash functions, key exchange, and randomness generation.

Post-quantum work covers the lattice-based and hash-based signature families, and several articles dissect concrete failures — an entropy defect in a hardware RNG, the security model of a hardware wallet's firmware.
