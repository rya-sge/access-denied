--- layout: category-list title: Post-Quantum Cryptography category: post-quantum permalink: /category/post-quantum/ description: "Post-quantum cryptography: the NIST standards ML-KEM, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA, lattice and hash-based foundations, implementations and quantum risk analyses." ---

Articles whose subject is post-quantum cryptography: the algorithms designed to resist an adversary with a large quantum computer, and the hardness assumptions they rest on. This covers the NIST standards (ML-KEM for key encapsulation, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA for signatures), the lattice problems behind them such as SIS and Module-LWE, and hash-based one-time signatures like Winternitz.

The category also holds implementation walkthroughs of these standards and analyses of how a whole protocol stands against Shor's and Grover's algorithms. An article that only has a section on quantum resistance, within a broader subject, stays in its own category.
