A systematic walk through the ISO 20022 standard, one message set at a time: payments initiation and clearing, securities settlement and reporting, card transactions from terminal to acquirer, and trade services.

The series starts with a map of the standard as a whole and then takes each four-letter business area in turn — pain, pacs, camt, caaa, catm, tsin, tsrv, tsmt and the rest — describing what each message does and when it is sent.
