A blockchain cannot see outside itself, so every protocol that needs a price, a rate or a risk parameter has to trust something that can. This category covers how that trust is structured.

The current article traces the move from plain price feeds to a unified risk layer with protocol-owned risk oracles. Oracle security also comes up throughout the DeFi and blockchain categories.
