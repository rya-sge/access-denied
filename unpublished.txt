Unpublished article registry
============================

Every article under _posts/ that carries `published: false` is listed here,
with why it is withheld and what releasing it requires. Jekyll excludes such
posts from site.posts (home, categories, series box, related posts,
search.json, feed, sitemap) and does not generate their URL.

An article listed here must have `published: false` in its front matter, and
every `published: false` post must be listed here; the check in the
create-article-no-publication skill verifies both directions.

Release = remove `published: false`, add `series:` if intended, add the
reciprocal links listed under `reciprocal:`, refresh `date:` if the content
was updated, run the create-article checks, then delete the entry below.

Diagram sources are registered in tree.txt, not here.

Entry format (indented here so it is not read as an entry; real entries
start in column 0):

    _posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md
      status:      unpublished (published: false)
      since:       YYYY-MM-DD
      reason:      <why it is not published>
      release:     <condition or date, or "undecided">
      series:      <intended series key, or none>
      reciprocal:  <_posts files that should link to it at release, or none>
      notes:       <anything the releaser must know; "none" if nothing>
